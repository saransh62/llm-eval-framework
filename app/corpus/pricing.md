# Acme Cloud Pricing

Acme Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Pro and Enterprise.

## Starter
The Starter plan costs $0 per month and includes 2 vCPUs, 4 GB of RAM and 20 GB of block storage.
Starter projects are limited to one team member and community support only.

## Pro
The Pro plan costs $49 per month per project. It includes 8 vCPUs, 32 GB of RAM and 500 GB of block storage.
Pro supports up to 10 team members and includes email support.

## Enterprise
Enterprise pricing is custom and starts at $2,000 per month with an annual contract.
Enterprise includes unlimited team members, SSO and a dedicated technical account manager.

## Discounts
Annual billing on the Pro plan gives a 15% discount. Registered non-profits receive a 30% discount on Pro.
Acme Cloud does not offer discount codes or coupons.
