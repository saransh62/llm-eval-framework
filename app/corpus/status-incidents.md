# Status Page and Incident Communication

## Status page
Live service status is published at status.acmecloud.example. Customers can subscribe to updates by email or webhook.

## Incident updates
During an active incident, Acme Cloud posts an update on the status page at least every 30 minutes.
A written post-incident report is published within 5 business days for any Severity 1 incident.

## Maintenance windows
Scheduled maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC and is announced at least 72 hours in advance.
