# Data Retention and Deletion

## Backups
Automated backups are taken daily and retained for 7 days on Pro and 35 days on Enterprise.
Starter projects are not backed up.

## Account deletion
When an account is deleted, customer data is permanently erased within 30 days.
Backups containing the data expire according to the normal backup retention schedule.

## Logs
Application logs are retained for 14 days. Audit logs are retained for 1 year on Enterprise.

## Data export
Customers can export all project data at any time from Settings > Export in JSON or CSV format.
