# Teams and Roles

## Roles
Acme Cloud has three roles: Owner, Admin and Viewer.
Owners manage billing and can delete the project. Admins can deploy and manage resources but cannot change billing.
Viewers have read-only access.

## Inviting members
Owners and Admins can invite members from Settings > Team. Invitations expire after 7 days.

## Transferring ownership
A project must always have at least one Owner. Ownership can be transferred by an existing Owner from Settings > Team > Transfer ownership.
