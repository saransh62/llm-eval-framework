# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
New Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the first payment.
After 14 days, monthly payments are non-refundable.

## Annual plans
Annual plans cancelled within 30 days of purchase receive a prorated refund for unused full months.
After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable but remain active until the end of the term.

## How to request
Refunds are requested from Billing > Invoices > Request refund. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms in the signed order form.
