# Regions and Availability

Acme Cloud runs in four regions: us-east (Virginia), us-west (Oregon), eu-central (Frankfurt) and ap-south (Mumbai).

## Data residency
Customer data stays in the region selected when the project is created. Data is not replicated across regions unless cross-region replication is enabled.

## Cross-region replication
Cross-region replication is available on Enterprise only and costs an extra $0.02 per GB transferred.

## Changing regions
A project's region cannot be changed after creation. To move, create a new project in the target region and migrate the data.
