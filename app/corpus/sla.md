# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Uptime commitments
The Pro plan has a monthly uptime commitment of 99.9%. The Enterprise plan has a monthly uptime commitment of 99.99%.
The Starter plan has no uptime commitment.

## Service credits
If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits:
- Below the commitment but at or above 99.0%: 10% credit of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.0%: 25% credit of the monthly fee.

Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month by opening a ticket with the subject "SLA credit".
Credits are applied to future invoices and are never paid out as cash.

## Exclusions
Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime.
