# Support

## Support channels by plan
Starter customers have access to the community forum only.
Pro customers get email support with a first response time of 1 business day.
Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone and email support with a first response time of 1 hour for Severity 1 issues.

## Severity levels
Severity 1 means production is down. Severity 2 means production is impaired. Severity 3 covers questions and minor issues.

## Support hours
Business-day support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 UTC.
