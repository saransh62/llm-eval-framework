# Partner Program

## Overview
The Acme Partner Program is for agencies and consultancies that build on Acme Cloud.
Partners earn a 20% revenue share on referred customers for the first 12 months.

## Requirements
To join, a partner must have at least 3 active customer projects on the Pro plan or higher and complete the Acme Certified Developer exam.

Partners are Silver until they refer 10 customers, then become Gold. Gold partners receive a dedicated partner manager.
