# Compliance

## Certifications
Acme Cloud holds a SOC 2 Type II report and ISO 27001 certification. The SOC 2 report is renewed every 12 months.
Customers can request the SOC 2 report under NDA from the Trust Center.

## GDPR
Acme Cloud acts as a data processor under GDPR and offers a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) to all customers.
The eu-central region is recommended for customers who need EU data residency.

## HIPAA
Acme Cloud is HIPAA eligible on the Enterprise plan only, after signing a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

## PCI DSS
Acme Cloud is not PCI DSS certified. Customers should not store raw card numbers on the platform.
