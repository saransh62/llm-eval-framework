# Billing and Invoices

## Billing cycle
Monthly plans are billed on the same calendar day each month as the original sign-up date.
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are available under Billing > Invoices.

## Payment methods
Acme Cloud accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Enterprise customers can pay by bank transfer with net-30 terms.
Acme Cloud does not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, Acme Cloud retries the charge 3 times over 7 days.
Accounts with an unpaid balance after 14 days are suspended until the balance is paid.

## Taxes
Prices exclude VAT and sales tax, which are added based on the billing address.
